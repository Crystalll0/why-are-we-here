## Why Am I Learning to Program?

My simplest answer is that I want to understand enough to participate.

At this stage, learning programming means returning to a skill I never really learned. I briefly encountered basic coding in both middle school and high school, but I never progressed beyond and introductory level. Later, I studied Motion Media Design at SCAD and minored in Photography. Apart from occasionally using simple expressions in Adobe After Effects, such as looping animations, I had few reasons to work with code. Those expressions were simple but useful. Now during my master's studies, programming seems worth revisiting: AI can write code, yet I struggle to understand and make practical use of what such a useful tool produces.

## Communication Is More Effective in a Familiar Field

When working with design or visual content, my experience helps me develop practical ideas and anticipate whether they are achievable or what they might look like. Bringing this understanding into a conversation with AI allows me to develop an initial idea through repeated discussion, making it clearer and more logically structured. For me, this process of exploration and exchange is ofter more valuable than receiving an immediate answer.

When I understand the subject, I can explain what feels wrong, suggest revisions, or ask follow-up questions that challenge a response. My existing knowledge allows me to analyse and question a result rather than simply accept or reject it as a whole.

With code, this exchange becomes much weaker. AI can generate code, but I do not really understand how it works, where to make changes, or how to integrate it into a larger project. I can describe an unsatisfactory result without knowing what caused it. It feels as though a substantial part of the information needed for the conversation is missing, which often frustrates me. I hope learning programming can help fill this gap and give me a better understanding of what is possible.

## Learning More Effectively

In an unfamiliar field, many details remain unclear. Even when someone explains something patiently, alack of practical experience can leave me with unanswered questions.
